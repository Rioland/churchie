Windows Your computer

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Download for Windows.zip

Install

  1. Open the downloaded Churchie-win-x64.zip and choose Extract all.
  2. Open the extracted Churchie folder and double-click Churchie.exe.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because Churchie isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.
  4. To find it easily next time, right-click Churchie.exe and choose Pin to Start, or Send to → Desktop (create shortcut).

Mac Your computer

macOS 12 Monterey or newer

Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).zip Mac with Intel processor.zip

Not sure which? Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon; "Processor: Intel…" means Intel.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded zip if your browser didn't already. You'll get Churchie.app.
  2. Drag Churchie.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because Churchie isn't notarised by Apple yet. Click Done.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Churchie and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password.
  5. From then on, Churchie opens normally from Launchpad, Spotlight or the Dock.

On macOS 14 or older you can instead Control-click Churchie.app, choose Open, then Open again.

Linux Your computer

64-bit (x86-64) with a desktop

Download for Linux.tar.gz

Install

  1. Extract the download:
    tar -xzf Churchie-linux-x64.tar.gz
  2. Start Churchie:
    ./Churchie/Churchie
  3. If it doesn't start on Debian or Ubuntu, install these and try again:
    sudo apt install libice6 libsm6 libfontconfig1

What you'll need

A computer

Windows 10 or 11, macOS 12 or newer, or 64-bit Linux, with 4 GB of memory and about 300 MB of free space.

A screen for the congregation

A projector or TV connected as a second screen. You can practise with just one screen; the projector view opens in its own window.

No internet on Sunday

You only need internet to download Churchie. Songs, Bibles and pictures are kept on your computer.
