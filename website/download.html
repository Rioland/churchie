Download Churchie

Free for Windows, Mac and Linux. Everything Churchie needs is inside the download, so there's nothing else to install.

Choose your download below Version 0.3.0.

Windows Your computer

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Download for WindowsInstaller (.exe), 39 MB

Need it on a USB stick, or can't install programs on this computer? Get the portable version (.zip): extract it and double-click Churchie.exe.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded Churchie-Setup-win-x64.exe.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because Churchie isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.
  3. Click Next, Install, then Finish. No administrator password is needed.
  4. Churchie opens. Next time, find it in the Start menu, or use the desktop shortcut.

To remove it later: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Churchie → Uninstall. Your songs and settings stay on the computer in case you reinstall.

Mac Your computer

macOS 12 Monterey or newer

Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)Installer (.pkg), 48 MB Mac with Intel processorInstaller (.pkg), 51 MB

Not sure which? Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon; "Processor: Intel…" means Intel.

Rather drag the app into Applications yourself? Get Churchie.app as a .zip for Apple Silicon or Intel.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded .pkg file. The first time, macOS says it can't check it, because Churchie isn't notarised by Apple yet. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Churchie and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  3. The installer opens. Click Continue, then Install, enter your password, and click Close. Churchie is now in your Applications folder.
  4. Open Churchie from Launchpad, Spotlight or Applications. If macOS asks about Churchie again, do step 2 once more.

On macOS 14 or older you can instead Control-click the .pkg file, choose Open, then Open again.

Linux Your computer

64-bit (x86-64) with a desktop

Download for Linux.tar.gz, 47 MB

Install

  1. Extract the download:
    tar -xzf Churchie-linux-x64.tar.gz
  2. Start Churchie:
    ./Churchie/Churchie
  3. If it doesn't start on Debian or Ubuntu, install these and try again:
    sudo apt install libice6 libsm6 libfontconfig1

What you'll need

A computer

Windows 10 or 11, macOS 12 or newer, or 64-bit Linux, with 4 GB of memory and about 300 MB of free space.

A screen for the congregation

A projector or TV connected as a second screen. You can practise with just one screen; the projector view opens in its own window.

No internet on Sunday

You only need internet to download Churchie. Songs, Bibles and pictures are kept on your computer.

File checksums (SHA-256). Build Churchie from source. Next step: learn how to use Churchie.
